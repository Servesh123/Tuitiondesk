### How to Install the APK on Your Android Phone

1. Open your phone's **Files** or **Downloads** app.
2. Tap on `app-debug.apk`.
3. If Android shows a popup saying:
   > *"For your security, your phone is not allowed to install unknown apps from this source"*
4. Tap **Settings** on that popup and enable the toggle **"Allow from this source"** (for Chrome / your browser or Files app).
5. Tap **Back** and press **Install**.
6. When installation finishes, tap **Open** to launch **TuitionDesk**.
